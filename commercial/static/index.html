Deterministic decisions, with evidence

LedgerLane verifies totals in decimal cents and checks configured mappings. If a receipt has an unknown job, mismatched card owner, missing code, or a required authorization, it remains pending for humans rather than being silently coded.

Prototype only. No outbound accounting actions, payment processing or automatic approval. Use fictional test data; authentication and storage are not ready for production financial records.
